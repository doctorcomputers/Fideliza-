## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a reliable, civic-grade commerce infrastructure balancing municipal authority with vibrant retail engagement. Designed for local merchants, municipal administrators, and residents, the interface instills confidence through structured layouts, high legibility, and dependable visual hierarchy.

The aesthetic leans on **Modern Civic Corporate** with localized Mediterranean cues. It avoids ephemeral novelty in favor of clear institutional trust, accessible contrast ratios, and organized transactional flows. Visual warmth emerges strictly through intentional accent notes rather than decorative clutter, ensuring high-density dashboards, QR validation tools, and citizen stamp-passports remain frictionless under varying environmental conditions (such as high-glare seaside daylight).

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the maritime and mineral identity of the municipality while prioritizing WCAG AAA readability for critical civic actions:

- **Primary (`#0A3663` - Deep Mediterranean Sea Blue):** Represents civic stability, governance, and institutional trust. Used for primary navigation, core CTAs, key brand markers, and authoritative states.
- **Secondary (`#E06D53` - Terracotta / Salt Lagoon Pink):** Applied deliberately for engagement, loyalty milestones, active loyalty stamps, and promotional highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#0EA5E9` - Soft Marine Cyan):** Used for informational prompts, connectivity indicators, active digital pass state indicators, and auxiliary metric callouts.
- **Accent / Alert (`#F59E0B` - Sun Gold):** Reserved for rewards progression, tier badges, warning states, and high-value merchant incentive markers.
- **Neutral Surface & Text (`#0F172A` - Slate):** High-density text neutral ensuring legibility against crisp salt sand surfaces (`#F8FAFC`) and pure white functional card layers (`#FFFFFF`).

Surfaces maintain light mode as standard to guarantee exterior usability at outdoor market stalls and storefront counters.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on `Inter` across all structural layers to maximize visual order, tabular numerical alignment, and rapid comprehension. 

- **Headlines and Displays:** Set with negative letter-spacing for structural tightness in administrative headers, merchant stats, and program announcements.
- **Body:** Standard neutral letter-spacing with open counters for frictionless multilingual reading (Spanish, Valencian, English).
- **Tabular Numerics:** Monospaced figure sets (`tnum`) must be enforced for all currency amounts, QR token counters, transaction IDs, and RFM scores to preserve alignment across analytical data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with a fluid 12-column grid system on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Breakpoints:** Mobile (320px–639px), Tablet (640px–1023px), Desktop (1024px+).
- **Margins & Gutters:** Mobile views utilize a compact `1rem` outer canvas padding to preserve horizontal real estate for merchant listings and vouchers. Desktop spans wider `3rem` margins to keep analytical dashboards bounded and scannable.
- **Density Controls:** Merchant POS screens and terminal widgets default to a compact density (`space-sm` to `space-md` gaps), whereas citizen engagement views leverage `space-lg` and `space-xl` element separation to minimize cognitive load and eliminate mis-taps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a combined model of **tonal surface separation** and **subtle ambient shadows** to keep structural integrity sharp under direct light:

- **Level 0 (Flat/Base):** Background canvas `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card/Container Surfaces):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp 1px structural border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Ideal for standard merchant tables and lists.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Layers):** Subtle ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 54, 99, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(10, 54, 99, 0.04)`. Applied to active vouchers, loyalty pass tiles, and dropdown overlays.
- **Level 3 (Modal / QR Overlays):** Concentrated depth: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 54, 99, 0.16)`. Used for camera scanning interfaces, point-of-sale modal alerts, and digital QR tokens.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on **Level 2 Roundedness** (`0.5rem` / 8px standard radius) to balance institutional structure with modern digital design:

- **Standard Containers & Inputs:** `0.5rem` (8px) for input fields, dashboard cards, merchant metric boxes, and operational tables.
- **Large Panels & Vouchers:** `1rem` (16px) for citizen voucher hero cards and merchant reward summary units.
- **Badges & Micro-Indicators:** Full pill forms (`9999px`) are designated exclusively for RFM segments (e.g., "Frequent Resident", "Tourist"), dynamic tags, and verification statuses to contrast sharply against geometric cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Civic Action:** Deep Blue (`#0A3663`) fill, white text, 8px radius, minimum touch target of 48px height. Focus states utilize a 2px offset ring in `#0EA5E9`.
- **Secondary / Loyalty Action:** Terracotta (`#E06D53`) fill or outlined variant for rewards claim, coupon redemptions, and gamification interactions.
- **Merchant Quick Action:** Ghost borders with slate text, shifting to 10% primary blue tint on hover.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** 44px minimum height, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px slate-tinted border (`#CBD5E1`). Focus states apply a crisp 1px border of `#0A3663` and an auxiliary light cyan glow ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Solid geometric checkboxes (4px radius) and circle radios with high-contrast `#0A3663` fills when active.

### Cards & Analytical Widgets
- **Standard Card:** Surface white, 8px radius, subtle border. Inner padding adheres to `space-md` (16px) or `space-lg` (24px).
- **Merchant RFM Widget:** Compact metric panels featuring a subtle status indicator line (e.g., green for High-Value, amber for At-Risk), bold tabular stat numbers, and a labeled pill badge.

### Loyalty Stamp & Voucher Badges
- **Digital Stamp Card:** 4x2 or 5x2 grid of circular stamp nodes (36px). Unstamped states feature a dashed slate ring; stamped states transition to a solid `#E06D53` fill with an embossed civic anchor/sun icon.
- **QR Token Display:** Dedicated high-contrast white panel flanked by micro-perforations (ticket aesthetic), centered 200px dynamic SVG QR code, surrounded by countdown timer and cryptographic hash label.
